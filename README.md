# **🏥 Workers' Compensation Claims & Operational Performance Analytics**

**Role:** Business Analyst

An analysis of 100,000 workers' compensation claims across 8,000 members, transformed into an interactive Tableau dashboard that helps management understand workplace injury trends, financial exposure, and claims processing performance.

[🔗 View the Live Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/ajith.attikkachalil/viz/workers_compensation_claims/WorkersCompensationClaims?publish=yes)  
🗄️ **Dataset:** Workers' Compensation Claims Data  \- generated by [ChatGPT.](https://chatgpt.com/)

## **📌 Business Requirement**

The organisation needed better visibility into its workers' compensation claims to understand where financial losses were occurring, which workplace injuries were driving costs, and why certain claims were taking longer to process.

Without a centralised reporting solution, management faced challenges in identifying:

* Which injury causes contributed to the highest claim volumes and financial costs.  
* How much money remained outstanding across Open and Pending claims.  
* Which injury types resulted in longer employee recovery periods.  
* Which claims management teams experienced the greatest review delays.  
* Whether system issues contributed to increased claims rework.  
* Which injury causes were associated with longer claim closure and lodgement timelines.

## **🎯 Task Overview**

Transform raw workers' compensation claims data into a clean, decision-ready analytical solution by:

* Cleaning and preparing the dataset using Python.  
* Identifying key claims and financial performance indicators.  
* Analysing injury causes, claim costs, and return-to-work timelines.  
* Investigating operational inefficiencies across claims management teams.  
* Identifying relationships between system issues, rework, and processing delays.  
* Building an interactive Tableau dashboard to communicate findings and support business decisions.

## **⚙️ Action**

**Cleaned the data:** Used Python to clean and prepare the raw workers' compensation claims dataset, addressing data quality issues before analysis.

**Stored the data in SQL:** Loaded the cleaned dataset into SQL to enable structured querying and exploratory analysis.

**Analysed claims performance:** Investigated 100,000 claims across 8,000 members, focusing on:

* Total members and claim volumes.  
* Open, Pending, and Closed claims distribution.  
* Average and median incurred costs by claim status.  
* Outstanding settlement liabilities.  
* Injury causes contributing to the highest claim volumes and financial costs.  
* Injury costs across different member sectors.  
* Return-to-work timelines by injury type.  
* Claims review delays across different teams.  
* System issues and their relationship with claims rework.  
* Average time taken to close claims.  
* Delays between injury occurrence and claim lodgement.

**Built the dashboard:** Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to provide management with visibility into:

* **Claims Overview:** Total members, total claims, and overall claims status distribution.  
* **Financial Performance:** Incurred costs, average claim costs, and outstanding settlement liabilities.  
* **Injury Analysis:** Injury causes contributing to the highest claim volumes and financial costs.  
* **Sector Analysis:** Injury patterns and financial impact across different member sectors.  
* **Return-to-Work Analysis:** Median recovery timelines across injury types.  
* **Operational Performance:** Claims review delays across different management teams.  
* **System Issues & Rework:** Comparison of claims rework frequency between claims with and without system issues.  
* **Claims Resolution:** Average closure and lodgement timelines across injury causes.

## **📈 Business Recommendations**

**1\. Workplace Safety & Injury Prevention:** Prioritise targeted safety training and preventive measures for Manual Handling and Slips, Trips, and Falls, which are among the leading contributors to claim volumes and total incurred costs.

**2\. Financial Exposure Management:** Strengthen monitoring of Open and Pending claims, which represent approximately \$663.95 million in outstanding settlement reserves, to improve financial forecasting and settlement management.

**3\. Improve Claims Management Efficiency:** Prioritise process improvements and resource allocation within the Complex Claims team, which has the highest average review delay of approximately 16.16 days.

**4\. Address System Issues:** Investigate and resolve system-related problems contributing to increased claims rework. Claims with system issues have an average rework count of 2.66 compared with 0.65 for claims without system issues.

**5\. Improve Return-to-Work Outcomes:** Develop targeted recovery and return-to-work support strategies for employees experiencing Major Physical and Psychological injuries, which have median return-to-work periods of 63 and 55 days, respectively.

**6\. Streamline Claims Lodgement:** Investigate delays between injury occurrence and claim lodgement, with the objective of reducing the average reporting time and improving the overall claims management process.

**7\. Monitor Claims Closure Performance:** Review injury categories associated with longer closure timelines, particularly Exposure, Other, and Repetitive Movement injuries, to identify opportunities to improve claim resolution efficiency.
